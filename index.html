About James Sketch Tours

Art, travel, and exploration combined.

James Sketch Tours offers guided sketching experiences designed for beginners and experienced artists alike. Rather than rushing from location to location, we take time to observe, sketch, and connect with our surroundings.


Whether exploring coastal landscapes, urban streets, botanical gardens, or hidden local gems, each tour encourages creativity while building confidence in observational drawing.
